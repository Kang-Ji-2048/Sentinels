§1 · Observations

Every storm overflow in England

Nine water companies publish live overflow status. We pull all of them and keep the 157 that started spilling in the last 48 hours.

Checked against real rainfall

For each spill we look at the three nearest Environment Agency rain gauges within 10 km. Blue ones had rain nearby, so they're probably allowed.

The dry ones stand out

73 spills started when every nearby gauge had less than 0.25 mm of rain on each of the two days before. If even one gauge was wet, we let the spill go, so we're erring on the side of caution.

The case we follow

We'll take one of these dry-day spills and follow it all the way through the agents.

§2 · Methods

How it works

A Databricks job runs on a schedule and hands anything suspicious to a team of five agents. Every step is traced in MLflow, so you can see exactly what each one did.

The skeptic runs on a different model on purpose, so the agents can't all just agree with each other. Anything the evidence agent claims comes with a link to its source. And every verdict gets saved, so the sites that keep doing it start to show up.

2Investigator Claude

Builds the case that this was a breach, using the data and what we already know about the site.

3Skeptic different model

Tries to find an innocent reason: a faulty sensor, rain the gauges missed, a pump failure, a tidal outfall. Then scores how likely it is to be a real breach.

4Evidence agent Linkup

Searches the web for anything that backs up either side: local news, beach closures, past fines, works going on.

5Adjudicator Claude

Goes through the skeptic's objections one by one against the evidence, decides which hold up, and sets the final confidence.

6Drafter Claude

Writes up the evidence pack and a draft message to the Environment Agency. A person reads it before anything goes out.

§5 · Limitations

A dry-day spill is a candidate, not proof

  • The data is self-reported. Overflow status comes from the water companies' own monitors.
  • Rain gauges are sparse. A shower between gauges can make a legal spill look dry. That's why the skeptic exists and every case carries a confidence score.
  • Some permits allow dry-weather discharges. Only the Environment Agency can decide a breach.
  • No report is sent automatically. A human reads and approves every one.
